## Description

Lagos is the Capital region of Xoria. It is a wealthy land auspicious for trade on both land and sea, as well as raising cattle and growing crops. At its northern border can be found the [[Istishian Church]], original home of the High God of Water [[Istishia]]; its large gothic facade faces the northern-eastern waters, allowing for a beautiful view of the sunrise on its coast.
